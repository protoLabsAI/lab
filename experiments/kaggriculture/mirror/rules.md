# Rules summary for implementation

This is an engineering summary, not legal advice. The [complete live rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/kaggriculture/rules) control.

## Participation and submissions

- Use one Kaggle account only.
- Maximum team size is 5. A participant may belong to only one team; mergers must meet the size/submission-count limits and finish by the merger deadline.
- Maximum 5 submissions per day; up to 2 final submissions are scored.
- Do not privately share competition code or data outside the official team. Public code sharing must be available to all competitors through the competition's Kaggle forum/notebooks and use a commercial-use-permitting OSI-approved license.

## Data, software, and external tools

- Competition data access/use is listed as Apache 2.0, but the data must not be redistributed to people who have not accepted the rules.
- External data, models, LLMs, and tools are allowed when the participant has the required rights and they are reasonably accessible at minimal cost.
- Automated ML tools are permitted when their licenses allow full rule compliance.
- Open-source dependencies used in a submission must meet the rules' commercial-use requirements.

## Runtime behavior

- During an episode, the submission cannot pull in external information or send information out.
- Do not use unobserved RNG state, opponent files/processes, timing channels, credentials, or network access.
- Published simulator equations and visible opponent state are legitimate inputs. Implementation-version differences should be tested, documented, and not treated as hidden-data access.

## Winner obligations

- Winner solution/source license: CC BY 4.0, subject to the specific exceptions in the rules for commercially available software and incompatible external model/data licenses.
- Winners must provide a detailed reproducible methodology and code repository, may be asked to participate in a recorded discussion, and must complete eligibility/tax documentation.
- Prize money is split evenly within a team unless all members unanimously notify Kaggle of a different allocation before payment.

## Eligibility and legal terms

Participants generally must be registered and at least 18 or the age of majority. Sanctions/export-control exclusions and local laws apply. Competition-entity personnel may compete but cannot receive prizes. California law and Santa Clara County venue govern the rules.
