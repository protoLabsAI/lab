audio-tags — held-out recorder

For each prompt, record one VOICED take (normal speaking) and one WHISPERED take. Click each "Save All" button at the end to download a zip of all recordings + manifest.

Setup notes 
- Recordings are 16 kHz mono WAV (browser-permitting).
- Files saved as: prompt_{NN}_{voiced|whispered}.wav
- Manifest jsonl included in the zip.
- Drop the unzipped contents in /mnt/data/audio-tags/held_out/ on the GPU box.
